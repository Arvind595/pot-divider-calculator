Model

Ideal: V = VCCW + (θ / mechanical travel) × (VCW − VCCW)

Dead band d = (mechanical − electrical) / 2, split equally at each end.

k = clamp((θ − d) / electrical travel, 0, 1)

RCCW = Rend + k(R − 2Rend), RCW = R − RCCW

Vout = (VCW/RCW + VCCW/RCCW + Vref/RL) / (1/RCW + 1/RCCW + 1/RL), nodal solution at the wiper with the load returned to Vref.

Entering an output voltage solves this numerically for θ. The linearity band is ± tolerance × |VCW − VCCW| around the modelled curve. Wiper contact resistance and resistance tolerance are not modelled.
